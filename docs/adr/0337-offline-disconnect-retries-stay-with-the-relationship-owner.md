# ADR-0337: Offline disconnect retries stay with the relationship owner

Accepted for Environment E4 ([#4113](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4113)). Offline revocation must survive Host restart without turning ordinary writes into replayable messages. The application-defined Host Relationship owner therefore retains the exact disconnected generation, peer pin, pending acknowledgement and bounded retry budget in its existing Operational Database rows; its Fiber owns the retry timer and encrypted requests. Device Pairing still owns device revocation, and admission checks the canonical relationship before granting access.

Only idempotent retirement is retried, at most eight automatic attempts per budget, with backoff capped at one minute. Exhaustion remains pending and inspectable; explicit retry renews the bounded budget. Retain up to 256 relationship records and refuse new proposals rather than silently deleting pending facts or identity pins. This chooses safe capacity refusal over unbounded history or unsafe pruning.

The pending-only cancel action atomically refuses an active relationship before writing revocation or suppression state. It uses a distinct public action so an older Host refuses it instead of ignoring a condition. Human or peer disconnect persists automatic-enrollment suppression; failed-initiation rollback does not. A fresh explicitly approved generation clears suppression only when activated. An old disconnect or acknowledgement cannot affect a later generation. Independent device/account authorities, ordinary mutation uncertainty and Channel contents retain their existing owners. Client settings drafts register transient exact-Host save/discard capabilities solely to guard navigation and Human removal; they store neither credentials nor a second Settings Target.
