## en

- Appearance settings are one flat list of titled sections. Panel opacity, blur and the workspace gap use sliders with the value beside them, appearance recovery and layout reset are action rows, and a selected wallpaper or Bot icon is shown by its fill and a check instead of an accent border ([#4344](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4344)).

## zh

- 「界面设置」改成一层带标题的分组。面板不透明度、背景模糊和面板间距改用滑块并在旁边显示数值；恢复外观和重置布局改成动作行；选中的壁纸和 Bot 图标用底色和勾号表示，不再加强调色边框（[#4344](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4344)）。
