## en

- A desktop call keeps going when you minimize the Mac or Windows window, close it to the Dock or tray, or switch to another app. While the window is hidden, a notification says you're on a call with the Bot; click it to return to the chat and its call bar. Quitting the app hangs up the call cleanly ([#3424](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3424)).

## zh

- 在 Mac 或 Windows 桌面端通话时，最小化窗口、把窗口关到 Dock 或托盘、或切到别的应用，通话都会继续。窗口隐藏期间会有一条通知提示你正在和 Bot 通话，点按即可回到聊天和通话栏。退出应用时会正常挂断通话（[#3424](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3424)）。
