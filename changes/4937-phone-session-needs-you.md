## en

- On the phone, the Sessions list follows the Host's needs-you rule, so a Session whose last turn failed also needs you. The Host Owner sees native Session approvals and questions in Activity with the Session's name; tapping one, or its notification, opens the Session. Activity's filter adds **From**: All, Bot or Sessions. Settings → Sessions has **Session requests in Activity** to keep them out of Activity and notifications ([#4937](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4937)).

## zh

- 手机上的会话列表采用 Host 的「需要你」规则，上一轮失败的会话也会显示需要你。Host Owner 能在「活动」里看到原生会话的审批和提问，并显示会话名称；轻点它或它的通知会打开该会话。活动筛选新增「来源」：全部、Bot 或会话。设置 → 会话中的「会话请求进入活动」可以让它们不进活动、不发通知（[#4937](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4937)）。
