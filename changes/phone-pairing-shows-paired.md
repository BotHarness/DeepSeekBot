## en

- A phone waiting for approval on your computer no longer gets stuck. If its connection drops or goes quiet while it waits (for example when you switch away to approve), it reconnects with the same key and finishes pairing once you approve. Retrying the same pairing link after an error also works now instead of failing with "invalid offer". When pairing succeeds, the page shows "Paired" with a Done button instead of the approval spinner.

## zh

- 手机等待电脑批准时不会再卡住。等待期间连接断开或没有回应（比如切去电脑上批准时），手机会用同一把密钥自动重连，电脑批准后就能完成配对。出错后用同一个配对链接重试也能成功，不会再报「配对邀请无效」。配对成功后页面显示「已配对」和「完成」按钮，不再一直转圈。
