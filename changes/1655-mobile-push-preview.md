## en

- DeepSeekBot phone notifications now have two lines: the Channel name on top (or the PersonaBot's name for a DM), and below it what the PersonaBot needs plus a short preview, such as the command to approve, the question or the message. Private input requests still show no content ([#1655](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1655)).

## zh

- DeepSeekBot 的手机通知现在分两行：上面是频道名（私聊为 PersonaBot 名字），下面是 PersonaBot 需要什么，加上待批准的命令、问题或消息的简短预览。私密输入请求仍不显示内容（[#1655](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1655)）。
