## en

- The right sub panel follows the theme color and wallpaper like the other panels: its body no longer paints a fixed grey background, and its entry headers use the same header material as other panel headers ([#4431](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4431)).
- In light mode with a wallpaper, secondary text and icons such as channel previews, status lines and inactive rail icons are darker, so they stay readable over translucent panels ([#4431](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4431)).
- The Composer and the jump-to-latest button use the floating surface with a soft shadow, so they stay distinct from message cards and wallpapers. Messages now fade out just above the Composer instead of being cut at its edge and showing through below it ([#4431](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4431)).

## zh

- 右侧子面板和其他面板一样跟随主题色和壁纸：主体不再是固定的灰底，各条目的 header 也用和其他面板 header 相同的材质（[#4431](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4431)）。
- 浅色模式设置壁纸后，频道预览、状态行和未选中的 rail 图标等次要文字和图标会更深，在半透明面板上依然清楚（[#4431](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4431)）。
- 输入栏和「回到最新」按钮改用悬浮层底色加轻阴影，在消息卡片和壁纸上都能分清；消息在输入栏上方淡出，不会在输入栏边缘被硬切，也不会从输入栏下方透出来（[#4431](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4431)）。
