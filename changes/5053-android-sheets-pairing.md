## en

- On Android at large system text sizes, sheet titles such as "Pair with your computer" and "Customize computer" wrap instead of being cut off. The pairing fingerprint shows two groups per row, so each row can be compared at a glance. The step bar's main button grows when its label wraps ([#5053](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5053)).

## zh

- 在 Android 上使用大号系统文字时，“与你的电脑配对”“自定义电脑”等面板标题会换行显示，不再被截断。配对指纹每行显示两组，方便逐行比对。分步操作栏的主按钮在文字换行时会随之变高（[#5053](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5053)）。
