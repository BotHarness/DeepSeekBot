## en

- People who can review pairing can now change a paired person's Role from Lark, Slack or Discord with `/role @name <role>`, list the Roles they can assign with `/roles`, and see someone's Role with `/role @name`, without a Bot turn. In a group, mention the Bot first; lists and a person's Role are sent to you privately ([#5325](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5325), [#5405](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/5405)).

## zh

- 有配对审核权限的人现在可以在 Lark、Slack 或 Discord 里用「/角色 @某人 <角色>」修改已配对成员的角色，用「/角色」列出自己能分配的角色，用「/角色 @某人」查看某人的角色，不会触发 Bot 回复。在群里要先 @ Bot；角色列表和个人角色会私信发给你（[#5325](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5325)、[#5405](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/5405)）。
