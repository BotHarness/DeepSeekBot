## en

- Pinned in Settings has two connection cards by default, Connect your phone to this computer and Connect another computer, which open those connection steps directly; their pin ids are `pair-phone` and `join-computer`. Each step of connecting a phone also has **Copy prompt for a Bot**, which copies an instruction naming the `deepseekbot` commands for that step (`phone-route`, `device-offer`, `device-requests` and `device-approve-key`), so a Bot can do it after checking the fingerprint with you ([#4198](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4198)).

## zh

- 设置的「常用」页默认多了两张连接卡片：「用手机连接这台电脑」和「连接其他电脑」，点开直接进入对应的连接步骤；它们的固定 id 是 `pair-phone` 和 `join-computer`。连接手机的每一步还有「复制给 Bot 的提示词」，复制的指令写明这一步要用的 `deepseekbot` 命令（`phone-route`、`device-offer`、`device-requests`、`device-approve-key`），Bot 和你核对指纹后就能代你完成（[#4198](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4198)）。
