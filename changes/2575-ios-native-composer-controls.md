## en

- iOS chat actions now use native buttons and system symbols; dictation keeps its tap, hold, cancel and lock gestures with native touch recognition and cancels interrupted holds ([#2575](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2575)).

## zh

- iOS 聊天操作改用原生按钮和系统图标；听写保留点按、长按、取消和锁定操作，使用原生触摸识别，并在未锁定的长按被打断时取消听写（[#2575](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2575)）。
