## en

- Deleting a Bot archives its DMs and removes them from the chat list. The CLI can also archive DMs left behind by earlier deletions ([#4038](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4038)).

## zh

- 删除 Bot 时会归档其私聊，并从聊天列表移除。也可以用 CLI 归档旧版删除操作遗留的私聊（[#4038](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4038)）。
