## en

- A unique private message accepted during a schedule or Assignment report wake can receive its structured final reply. Local send receipts attribute that reply to the private request so `send` and `send-status` return the committed result. Background wakes do not supply a reply destination; multiple message sources and explicit send attempts still require explicit delivery tools.

## zh

- 定时或 Assignment 报告唤醒期间新接收的唯一私信可收到结构化最终答复。本地发送回执将答复归属该私信，`send` 和 `send-status` 可返回已提交结果。后台唤醒不提供回复目标；多个消息来源或已有显式发送尝试时仍需使用显式交付工具。
