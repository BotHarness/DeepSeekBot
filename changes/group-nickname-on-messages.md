## en

- Your nickname in a group is now shown as your name on all your messages in that group, including older ones and quoted replies, on the computer and the phone. Clear the nickname and they show your chat name again. On the phone, the group's member list now shows the people in the group with their nickname and photo, above the Bots ([#4446](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4446)).

## zh

- 设置了群昵称后，你在这个群里的所有消息（包括以前的消息和引用回复）都会显示群昵称，电脑和手机都一样；清除群昵称后恢复显示聊天名字。手机上的群成员列表现在也会列出群里的人，显示他们的群昵称和头像，排在 Bot 前面（[#4446](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4446)）。
