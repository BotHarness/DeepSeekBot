## en

- A Bot that sends work back to a colleague with @ wakes it on every round instead of only the first, so multi-round reviews between Bots no longer stall until a Human steps in; when a colleague still cannot be woken, the sending Bot is told and reports where the work stands. Bots also confirm receipt of longer tasks, report progress at milestones and finish with a result or explicit status ([#2872](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2872)).

## zh

- Bot 用 @ 把工作退回给同事时，每一轮都会唤醒对方，而不只是第一次；Bot 之间多轮返工不再停住等人来戳。若同事仍无法被唤醒，发送方会得到提示并说明当前进展。Bot 处理较长任务时也会先确认收到、在关键节点汇报进度，最后给出结果或明确状态（[#2872](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2872)）。
