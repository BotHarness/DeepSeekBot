## en

- Added the first Windows-to-Android Local Computer video path over the paired encrypted LAN channel, with explicit control and a Bot pause that survives release and restart; the video path has no image fallback. Stalled video keeps Release and Reconnect available. Connect remains refused pending its later slice. Physical-device and Human qualification remain required; downgrade after takeover requires coordinated pause-preserving recovery ([English guide](docs/local-computer-video.md), [Chinese guide](docs/local-computer-video.zh.md), [#2558](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2558)).
