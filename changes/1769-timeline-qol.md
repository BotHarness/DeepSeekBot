## en

- Opening a conversation with unread replies now shows a New messages divider at your last-read point, switching conversations keeps the message you were reading, long messages fold behind Show all, each grouped message shows its own time on hover, and images open in an in-page preview you can step through with the arrow keys ([#1769](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1769)).

## zh

- 打开有未读回复的会话时，会在上次读到的位置显示「以下为新消息」分隔线；切换会话后回来仍停在原来读到的消息；过长的消息默认折叠，可点「展开全部」；同组的每条消息悬停时显示自己的时间；图片在页面内预览，可用左右方向键切换（[#1769](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/1769)）。
