## en

- The Host Owner can transfer the Steward role with `deepseekbot steward-transfer`. Both Human DMs record the change, and both Bots keep their Memory, Groups and Sessions ([#5193](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5193)).

## zh

- Host Owner 可通过 `deepseekbot steward-transfer` 转移主管角色。双方的 Human DM 会记录这次变更，两个 Bot 都会保留原有的 Memory、Group 和 Session（[#5193](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5193)）。
