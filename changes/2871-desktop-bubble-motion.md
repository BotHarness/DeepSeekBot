## en

- In the desktop app, Bot replies now appear once they are complete, with the bottom of the message list showing which Bots are typing meanwhile. Turn on **Settings → Messaging → Stream Bot replies as they are written** (or `streamReplies` through `deepseekbot settings-set botharness-client`) to see the text as it is written. New messages scale into the list, and the Bot working row at the bottom of the list now expands and collapses smoothly instead of pushing the list ([#2871](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2871)).

## zh

- 桌面端 Bot 回复改为写完整后再显示，写作期间在消息列表底部显示哪些 Bot 正在输入。如需逐字查看，可在**设置 → 消息 → 逐字显示 Bot 回复**开启（或通过 `deepseekbot settings-set botharness-client` 设置 `streamReplies`）。新消息出现时有缩放入场动画；列表底部的 Bot 工作状态改为平滑展开和收起，不再让列表跳动（[#2871](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2871)）。
