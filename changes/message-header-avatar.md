## en

- On desktop, click the Bot's avatar and name in the Messages header to open its Profile; the separate profile button is gone, and the subpanel column button now sits at the far right of the header ([#3092](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/3092)).
- Right-click a Bot in the Channel list and choose Change avatar… to edit its avatar without opening its Profile. In the Profile, hovering or focusing the avatar now shows an edit overlay ([#3092](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/3092)).

## zh

- 桌面端点击「消息」面板标题里的 Bot 头像和名称即可打开它的资料页；单独的资料按钮已移除，子面板栏按钮移到标题栏最右侧（[#3092](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/3092)）。
- 在频道列表中右键点击 Bot，选择「修改头像…」即可直接编辑头像，无需先打开资料页。资料页中，鼠标悬停或键盘聚焦头像时会显示编辑遮罩（[#3092](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/3092)）。
