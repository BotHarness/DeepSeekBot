## en

- Scrolling a long chat history on desktop is smoother: mounting messages no longer restyles the whole window, so frames over 25 ms while wheel-scrolling a 600-message Group dropped from about 18% to about 3%.

## zh

- 桌面端滚动较长的聊天历史更流畅了：挂载消息时不再重新计算整个窗口的样式，在 600 条消息的群聊里用滚轮滚动时，超过 25ms 的帧从约 18% 降到约 3%。
