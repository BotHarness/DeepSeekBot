## en

- On iOS and Android, swiping left on Chats now brings back the last chat you were in, sliding in from the right as in Slack, so returning to the Bot or Channel you just left takes one swipe. Swiping right still opens the workspaces. Conversation rows no longer swipe; pin, section, mute and read stay in the long-press menu ([#3055](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/3055)).

## zh

- 在 iOS 和 Android 的 Chats 列表上左滑，会把上次进入的聊天从右侧推回来（参考 Slack），回到刚才那个 Bot 或 Channel 只需一滑；右滑仍然打开环境切换。会话行不再支持左右滑，置顶、分组、免打扰和标为已读都保留在长按菜单里（[#3055](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/3055)）。
