## en

- On Android at large system text sizes, the conversation header keeps Back, ⋮, call and the message selection actions at their normal size. The Bot or group name now wraps to two lines instead of shrinking to "Ada …", and Share stays on screen in selection mode ([#5053](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5053)).

## zh

- 在 Android 上使用大号系统文字时，对话顶部的返回、⋮、通话以及多选操作按钮保持正常大小。Bot 或群组名称改为最多换成两行显示，不再缩成“Ada …”；多选模式下的分享按钮也不再被挤出屏幕（[#5053](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5053)）。
