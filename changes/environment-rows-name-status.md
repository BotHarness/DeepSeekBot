## en

- On iOS and Android, each environment in the environment list shows its name and its online status on one line each, with the unread or Needs you count on the corner of its icon and ⋯ at the end for its settings. Connection addresses now appear only on the environment's Connection page ([#3571](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/3571)).

## zh

- iOS 和 Android 的环境列表中，每个环境只显示两行：名称和在线状态，各占一行；未读或待处理的数量显示在图标右上角，最右侧的 ⋯ 打开设置。连接地址只在该环境的「连接」页面显示（[#3571](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/3571)）。
