## en

- Reading a Bot's reply on one device now clears its notification on the others: the phone removes reply notifications and in-app banners you already read on the computer, and the web app closes its notices for requests and replies handled on the phone. The Host also looks through up to 200 unread replies, so an older unread reply is no longer cleared as if it had been read.

## zh

- 在一台设备上读过 Bot 的回复后，其他设备上的通知会自动清掉：手机会移除你已经在电脑上读过的回复通知和 App 内横幅，网页端也会关掉已经在手机上处理过的请求和回复通知。Host 现在会检查最多 200 条未读回复，较早的未读回复不再被误当作已读清掉。
