## en

- You can now call a Bot from Android too, with the same call button, call screen and setup as on iPhone. The call uses the phone's echo cancellation so the Bot's reply on the speaker is not heard back as you, it ends when you leave the app or lock the phone, and if microphone access is off the call screen lets you allow it again or open Settings ([#3405](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3405)).

## zh

- 现在也可以在 Android 上给 Bot 打电话，通话按钮、通话界面和设置都和 iPhone 一样。通话会使用手机的回声消除，Bot 在外放里的回复不会被当成你说的话；离开 App 或锁屏时通话会结束；如果麦克风权限关闭，通话界面可以让你重新允许或打开设置（[#3405](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3405)）。
