## en

- On iPhone, when iOS gives the app background time, it brings saved chats up to date from each paired computer. Chats that changed while you were away then open without waiting for the computer. It reads at most two pages per chat and skips the run in Low Power Mode or when the battery is low and unplugged ([#3581](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3581)).

## zh

- 在 iPhone 上，iOS 给 app 后台时间时，app 会从每台已配对的电脑把已保存的聊天同步到最新。你不在时有变化的聊天，之后打开就不用等电脑。每个聊天最多读取两页；低电量模式下，或电量低且未充电时，会跳过这次同步（[#3581](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3581)）。
