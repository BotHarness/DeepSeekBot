## en

- The Sessions panel shows each Session's DSH goal and to-do progress (for example 3/6), uses the current to-do as the row's status line, and moves a running Session with no new event for 10 minutes into a Stuck section. Clicking a Session opens its full conversation beside the messages, with the goal and to-dos as chips that expand in place, and a composer that sends a message straight into an Assignment while the Orchestrator gets an Inbox notice. `deepseekbot session-progress` and `deepseekbot session-transcript` read the same data ([#4388](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/4388)).

## zh

- 会话面板显示每个会话的 DSH 目标和任务进度（例如 3/6），用当前任务作为这一行的进展，运行中但 10 分钟没有新事件的会话会归到「卡住了」分组。点击会话会在消息旁边打开完整对话：目标和任务以 chip 显示，点开在原地展开；输入框把消息直接发进 Assignment，同时 Orchestrator 会在 Inbox 收到通知。`deepseekbot session-progress` 和 `deepseekbot session-transcript` 读取同样的数据（[#4388](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/4388)）。
