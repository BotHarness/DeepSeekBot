## en

- Send `/pair` to your Bot in a Discord DM to ask for management pairing. Once you approve it in Web and route approvals to it, tool approvals and Bot questions reach that Discord DM as text: reply `allow` or `reject` (or `#REF allow`), or the letter of a question's option. Needs dsh-im with Discord DM support ([#5305](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5305)).

## zh

- 在 Discord 私聊里给 Bot 发 `/pair` 即可申请管理配对。在 Web 批准并把审批路由指向它之后，工具审批和 Bot 的提问会以文字发到这个 Discord 私聊：回复「允许」或「拒绝」（或 `#REF 允许`），提问则回复选项字母。需要支持 Discord 私聊的 dsh-im（[#5305](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5305)）。
