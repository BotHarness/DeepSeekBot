## en

- Phone Chats rows now reveal Pin, Add to section and Mute by swiping, with the same actions in an accessible menu. Choose or create a section on the conversation's Remote Host; retrying a failed assignment reuses the created section, and switching Hosts refuses stale actions. iOS keeps its left-edge drawer gesture and chat preview ([#2519](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2519)).

## zh

- 手机聊天行支持滑动显示置顶、添加到分区和静音，也可通过无障碍菜单操作。在会话所属的远程 Host 选择或新建分区；添加失败后重试会复用已创建的分区，切换 Host 后拒绝旧操作。iOS 保留左边缘抽屉手势和聊天预览（[#2519](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2519)）。
