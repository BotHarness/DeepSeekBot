## en

- Manage a remote Channel’s owning Bot from its chat: rename it, choose its model or preset, authorize or revoke its folders, and select its approval template on the original Host. Unsaved names stay with that Bot until saved or discarded ([#4118](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4118)).
