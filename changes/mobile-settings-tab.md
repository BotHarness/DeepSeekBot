---
section: Changed
---

## en

- On iOS and Android, Settings in the bottom bar is now its own tab page, like Chats and Activity, instead of a sheet over the current tab. Its pages (Account, Usage, Models, Notifications, Storage and more) open as full pages with Back.

## zh

- 在 iOS 和 Android 上，底部栏的“设置”改为与“聊天”“动态”一样的独立标签页，不再以弹层覆盖当前页面。其中的各个页面（账户、用量、模型、通知、存储等）以完整页面打开，可返回。
