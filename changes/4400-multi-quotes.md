## en

- Quote several messages, or only part of one, in a single message: select text in a message and right-click "Quote selection", or choose "Quote" for the whole message. Each quote waits as a chip in the message box, and the sent message shows them above its text; Reply still works as before. Bots see what you quoted and can cite several earlier messages in their own replies (`bot_send_message` `quotes`; CLI `deepseekbot send --quote`) ([#4400](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4400)).

## zh

- 一条消息可以引用多条消息，或只引用某条消息的一部分：在消息里选中文字后右键「引用所选内容」，或选「引用」引用整条。每条引用在输入框里显示为一个 chip，发出后显示在正文上方；「回复」照旧可用。Bot 能看到你引用的内容，也能在回复中引用多条历史消息（`bot_send_message` 的 `quotes`；CLI `deepseekbot send --quote`）（[#4400](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4400)）。
