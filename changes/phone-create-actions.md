## en

- Open the Chats creation card from the floating + button to start a Group or Bot. Host Owners can open the New Session screen from Sessions. The Sessions list starts at the same position in English and Chinese ([#4974](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4974)).

## zh

- 点按「聊天」中的悬浮加号，打开创建卡片并新建群聊或 Bot。Host 所有者可从「会话」打开新建会话页面。中英文会话列表的起始位置一致（[#4974](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4974)）。
