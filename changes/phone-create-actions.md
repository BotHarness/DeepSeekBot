## en

- Create a Group or Bot from the Chats navigation bar on iOS or the floating action button on Android. Host Owners can open the New Session screen from Sessions. The Sessions list starts at the same position in English and Chinese ([#4974](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4974)).

- iOS 可从「聊天」导航栏新建群聊或 Bot，Android 可使用悬浮操作按钮。Host 所有者可从「会话」打开新建会话页面。中英文会话列表的起始位置一致（[#4974](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4974)）。
