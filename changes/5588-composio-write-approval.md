## en

- Composio writes now wait for Tool Approval, including writes batched with reads. Rejected or expired approvals send nothing to Composio ([#5588](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5588)).

## zh

- Composio 写入操作现在必须先获得工具审批，读写混合的批量调用也一样。审批被拒绝或过期时，Host 不会向 Composio 发出调用（[#5588](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5588)）。
