## en

- Connect encrypts messages, attachments, approvals and private inputs with Noise XK after independent Host fingerprint comparison and explicit device-key approval. Untrusted enrollment addresses are refused, notification approvals stop after Connect errors, and notification settings use the encrypted channel. Pairing shows concise native verification steps with complete grouped fingerprints and optional explanations. The phone gains a native private-input form, and iOS pairing returns to usable Chats without a cold restart, including immediately after dismissing the first-use notice; older Connect pairings require pairing again, and Browser/Computer viewers remain unavailable on Connect pending qualification ([#2542](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2542)).

## zh

- Connect 在独立核对 Host 指纹并明确批准设备公钥后，通过 Noise XK 加密消息、附件、审批与私密输入。不可信的配对地址会被拒绝，通知审批遇到 Connect 错误会停止，通知设置通过加密通道读取。配对改用简洁的原生分步界面，完整指纹分组显示，说明按需展开。手机新增原生私密输入表单，iOS 配对后可直接使用聊天，无需冷启动，即使刚关闭首次使用说明也不阻塞点击；旧 Connect 配对需要重新配对，Browser/Computer 查看器在 Connect 上暂不可用，等待单独验收（[#2542](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2542)）。
