## en

- Connect encrypts messages, attachments, approvals and private inputs with Noise XK after independent Host fingerprint comparison and explicit device-key approval. The phone gains a native private-input form; older Connect pairings require pairing again, and Browser/Computer viewers remain unavailable on Connect pending qualification ([#2542](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2542)).

## zh

- Connect 在独立核对 Host 指纹并明确批准设备公钥后，通过 Noise XK 加密消息、附件、审批与私密输入。手机新增原生私密输入表单；旧 Connect 配对需要重新配对，Browser/Computer 查看器在 Connect 上暂不可用，等待单独验收（[#2542](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2542)）。
