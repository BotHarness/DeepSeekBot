## en

- On mobile, opening a reply or mention from Activity now opens its chat at that message and marks it read, instead of showing a read-only page that left it unread ([#3322](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3322)).

- 移动端从「活动」点开回复或 @提及时，现在会直接打开对应聊天并定位到该消息，同时标记为已读；此前会进入只读页面，未读状态一直不消失（[#3322](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3322)）。
