## en

- Settings → Account now has a **My uploaded Bots** entry when you are signed in. On the computer it opens the list of Bots you uploaded to the Marketplace, where you can update or delete them, and an open Market panel updates right away. On the phone, Profile → My uploaded Bots lists them and lets you view or delete each one ([#4340](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4340)).

## zh

- 登录后，设置 → 账号里新增「我上传的 Bot」入口。在电脑上可以打开你上传到市场的 Bot 列表，在这里更新或删除，已打开的市场面板会同步更新。在手机上，个人资料 → 我上传的 Bot 会列出这些 Bot，可以查看或删除（[#4340](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4340)）。
