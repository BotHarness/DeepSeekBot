## en

- Settings → Account now has a **My uploaded Bots** entry when you are signed in. It opens the list of Bots you uploaded to the Marketplace, where you can update or delete them; an open Market panel updates right away ([#4340](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4340)).

## zh

- 登录后，设置 → 账号里新增「我上传的 Bot」入口，可以打开你上传到市场的 Bot 列表，在这里更新或删除；已打开的市场面板会同步更新（[#4340](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4340)）。
