## en

- A Session's message box is now a single-line pill on the phone and the desktop, giving the conversation more room. The model shows as its logo beside Send; once the draft wraps, the model name and reasoning level come back next to the send button. Tap the logo, or "Model" in the Session title menu, to pick a model in a sheet; a pick still applies from the next turn ([#5214](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5214)).

## zh

- 会话的输入框在手机和桌面上都改成单行胶囊，给对话留出更多空间。模型以图标形式显示在发送按钮旁；草稿换行后，模型名称和推理强度会回到发送按钮旁边。点模型图标，或会话标题菜单里的“模型”，即可在面板中选择模型；选择仍从下一轮开始生效（[#5214](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5214)）。
