## en

- Alt+K now searches messages across every Bot DM and group you can see, not only conversation names and settings. Matching messages appear under Messages with the conversation, who wrote it, a highlighted snippet and when it was sent; Enter opens the conversation at that message. Two-character Chinese words match too, attachment file names are searched, and hidden conversations stay out. On mobile, the Chats search lists the same message matches from every connected computer and opens the chat at the message. Agents use `deepseekbot message-search --query <text>`.

## zh

- Alt+K 现在除了会话名称和设置，还能搜索你可见的所有 Bot 私聊和群聊里的消息。匹配的消息显示在「消息」分组下，带会话、发送者、高亮片段和发送时间；回车打开会话并定位到那条消息。两个字的中文词也能搜到，附件文件名也会被搜索，已隐藏的会话不参与搜索。移动端 Chats 搜索同样列出所有已连接电脑上的匹配消息，点按后打开聊天并定位到该消息。Agent 可用 `deepseekbot message-search --query <text>`。
