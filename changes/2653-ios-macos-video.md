## en

- Watch a Mac's primary display from iPhone over the paired local network, explicitly take control to click, then hand control back to Bots. The native video receiver requires a rebuilt iOS app; failed video never falls back to screenshots ([#2653](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2653)).
