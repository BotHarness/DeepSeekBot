## en

- Mobile cloud notification settings reread system permission when returning to the app, recover registration after permission is restored, and stop reporting an old denied state. Healthy unchanged permission does not trigger duplicate registration ([#3361](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3361)).
