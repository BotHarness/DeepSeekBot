## en

- On iPhone, the app no longer runs encryption work for a saved Computer that is switched off or has moved; it starts the secure handshake only after the Computer answers, which saves CPU and battery when several old Computers are saved.

## zh

- 在 iPhone 上，已保存的电脑关机或换了端口时，App 不再为它做加密握手计算；只有电脑真正应答后才开始握手。保存了多台旧电脑时更省 CPU 和电量。
