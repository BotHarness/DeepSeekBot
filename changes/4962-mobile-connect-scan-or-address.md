## en

- The iOS and Android Pair screen now has two ways in: scan or enter, and Tobs account sign-in. One field accepts a pairing link, an invitation link or a Host address. For an address, the phone shows the Host's fingerprint beside one username and password form, and the Host decides whether you sign in as its Owner or a member ([#4962](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4962)).

## zh

- iOS 和 Android 的配对页只保留两条路：扫码或输入，以及登录 Tobs 账号。同一个输入框可以填配对链接、邀请链接或 Host 地址；输入地址时，手机在同一屏显示 Host 指纹和用户名、密码表单，由 Host 判断你以 Owner 还是成员身份登录（[#4962](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4962)）。
