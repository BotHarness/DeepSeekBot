## en

- Screenshots in Report a problem now work like a capture session: the report steps aside and a floating toolbar stays at the bottom while you scroll or switch pages, each Screenshot opens markup, and Add to attachments puts it in the report; Done brings the report back. Markup gains text comments (under a box or anywhere you click) and a one-line note per image that goes into the report description. Toolbar thumbnails reopen a capture with its marks still editable or copy it to the clipboard, and the report can download just its images. The Desktop app, which cannot share its screen, now captures the page itself instead of failing silently ([#4003](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/4003)).

## zh

- “报告问题”里的截图改为截图模式：报告窗口先收起，底部悬浮一条工具栏，你可以滚动或切换页面，每次点“截图”后先标注，再点“添加到附件”放进报告，点“完成”回到报告。标注新增文字批注（框选后或任意位置点击即可写），每张图还能写一句话说明，会写进报告描述。工具栏的缩略图可以点开重新修改标注，也能直接复制到剪贴板；报告里还可以只下载截图。桌面端没法共享屏幕，现在会直接截取页面本身，不再点了没反应（[#4003](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/4003)）。
