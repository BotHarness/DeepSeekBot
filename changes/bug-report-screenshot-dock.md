## en

- Screenshots in Report a problem now work like a capture session: the report steps aside and a floating toolbar stays at the bottom while you scroll or switch pages, each Screenshot opens markup and Add to attachments puts it in the report, and Done brings the report back. Several screenshots are fine, and the Desktop app, which cannot share its screen, now captures the page itself instead of failing silently.

## zh

- “报告问题”里的截图改为截图模式：报告窗口先收起，底部悬浮一条工具栏，你可以滚动或切换页面，每次点“截图”后先标注，再点“添加到附件”放进报告，可以截多张，点“完成”回到报告。桌面端没法共享屏幕，现在会直接截取页面本身，不再点了没反应。
