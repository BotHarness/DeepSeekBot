## en

- Android navigation slides down while scrolling downward; the creation FAB shrinks and fades from its bottom center. Scrolling upward restores both, and screen readers keep both visible. ([#2669](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2669))

- Android Computer slows click feedback, uses click-only mouse buttons and continuous held scrolling in the central pad, and shows keyboard/reset icons after takeover in a native Material toolbar. Chats and Activity return to native navigation with horizontal labels; Activity keeps its icon and shows pending counts in its label. ([#2559](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2559), [#2669](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2669))

## zh

- Android 向下滚动时导航栏下移隐藏，新建 FAB 以底边中点为支点缩小并淡出；向上滚动恢复，开启屏幕阅读器时两者始终可见。([#2669](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2669))

- Android Computer 放慢点击波纹，左右键只负责点击，中间滚动区域支持按住持续滚动；接管后在原生 Material 工具栏显示键盘和归位图标。聊天与动态恢复原生导航栏，图标和标签横向排列，动态保留图标并在标签中显示待处理数量。([#2559](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2559), [#2669](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2669))
