## en

- Android Computer slows click feedback, uses click-only mouse buttons and continuous held scrolling in the central pad, and shows keyboard/reset icons after takeover in a native Material toolbar. Chats and Activity return to native navigation with horizontal labels; Activity keeps its icon and shows pending counts in its label. ([#2559](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2559), [#2669](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2669))
