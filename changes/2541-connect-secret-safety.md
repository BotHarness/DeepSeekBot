## en

- Connect refuses private input and known credential-setting methods until an encrypted channel is available. Remote chat explains how to enter secrets on the Host or through Tailscale. The phone's full Host WebView and its entry points are removed on every route; native screens remain. Thanks to Vain for identifying the trust boundary ([#2541](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2541)).

## zh

- Connect 在提供加密通道前拒绝私密输入及已知的凭据设置方法，远程聊天提示在本机或通过 Tailscale 填写密钥。手机内的完整 Host WebView 及入口在所有路由上移除，保留原生页面。感谢 Vain 指出信任边界问题（[#2541](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2541)）。
