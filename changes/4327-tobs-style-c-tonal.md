## en

- Appearance settings gain a third Style, Material, modelled on Material 3 Expressive. Panels, muted text and hover states carry a light tint of the accent colour (a custom colour too), settings rows become separate rounded tiles, section titles and the selected navigation item take a soft accent tone, controls are pill-shaped and a little larger, and chat text is set at 14px. Choose it under Appearance > Style or with `deepseekbot appearance-set` ([#4327](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4327)).

## zh

- 界面设置的「风格」新增第三种参考 Material 3 Expressive 的「Material」：面板底色、次要文字和悬停状态都带一点主题色（自定义主色也一样），设置行变成分开的圆角色块，分组标题和选中的导航项用柔和的强调色，控件改成胶囊形并略大，聊天文字为 14px。在「界面设置 › 风格」里选择，也可以用 `deepseekbot appearance-set` 设置（[#4327](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4327)）。
