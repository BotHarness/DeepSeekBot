## en

- The Android composer clears hold-to-record hints when recognition ends or cannot start, even if the microphone button disappears before release; locked recording continues until stopped ([#3233](https://github.com/BotHarness/BotHarness/issues/3233)).

## zh

- Android 输入栏会在语音识别结束或无法启动时清除长按录音提示，即使麦克风按钮在松手前已消失；锁定录音仍会持续到主动停止（[#3233](https://github.com/BotHarness/BotHarness/issues/3233)）。
