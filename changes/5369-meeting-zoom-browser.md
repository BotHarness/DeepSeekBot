## en

- A Bot sent to a Zoom meeting now joins through the Zoom web client in its own Chrome window on the Host, named after the Bot and its Owner. It hears every participant from inside the page, without using the computer's speakers or microphone, never makes a sound in the meeting, and leaves when the meeting ends or everyone else has left. The Host needs Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge ([#5369](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5369)).

## zh

- 派去 Zoom 会议的 Bot 现在会在 Host 上用独立的 Chrome 窗口通过 Zoom 网页版入会，显示名带上 Bot 和主人的名字。它在页面内听到每位参会者，不占用电脑的扬声器和麦克风，在会上不发出任何声音，会议结束或其他人都离开后自动退出。Host 需要安装 Google Chrome 或 Microsoft Edge（[#5369](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5369)）。
