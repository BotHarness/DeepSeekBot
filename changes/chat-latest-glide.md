## en

- When a chat is at the newest message, sending a message or a new Bot message now glides the view smoothly to the bottom on iOS and Android, and on iOS streamed replies keep gliding in step with the screen, instead of trailing a few frames behind and restarting the scroll on every line ([#3850](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/3850)).

## zh

- 聊天停在最新消息时，iOS 和 Android 上发送消息或 Bot 新消息会让画面原生平滑地滑到底部，iOS 的流式回复也同样跟随，与屏幕刷新同步，不再慢几帧、每增加一行就重新开始滚动（[#3850](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/3850)）。
