## en

- The iOS and Android Pair menu now lists Private connection and account sign-in first, then joining someone else's Host, then Advanced methods (same Wi-Fi, Tailscale and Owner sign-in), each with its own icon ([#4900](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4900)).

## zh

- iOS 和 Android 的配对菜单改为先列私有连接和账号登录，再列加入他人的 Host，最后是进阶方式（同一 Wi-Fi、Tailscale 和 Owner 登录），每种方式使用各自的图标（[#4900](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4900)）。
