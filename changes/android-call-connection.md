## en

- Android Bot calls keep their computer connection when you leave the app, even with notification “Keep connected” switched off; ending the call restores the normal background connection policy ([#3802](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3802)).

## zh

- Android Bot 通话在离开 App 后仍保持电脑连接，无需开启通知设置中的「保持连接」；通话结束后恢复原来的后台连接规则（[#3802](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3802)）。
