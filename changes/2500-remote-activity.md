## en

- Activity Center groups paired Remote Hosts' requests, replies and unread conversations by Host. Remote names and avatars stay scoped to their Host; approvals, questions, join requests, snooze and clear act on that Host, and source links open its conversation ([#2500](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2500)).
- Remote read and submission failures show a short translated retry hint while keeping loaded items and diagnostic causes. Decisions already handled on another device or expired do not count as a successful submission ([#2500](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2500)).
- Unreadable remote activity items show a retry hint while valid items remain usable; retrying a failed conversation open targets the same Host ([#2500](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2500)).
- Switching remote Hosts or Channels clears the previous conversation's unsent draft in Activity Center and the conversation panel, preventing it from being sent to the new destination ([#2500](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2500)).

## zh

- 活动中心按 Host 展示已配对远程的请求、回复和未读会话，使用各自的名称与头像；审批、问题、加入请求、稍后处理和清除均发送到对应 Host，来源链接打开其会话（[#2500](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2500)）。
- 远程读取和提交失败时保留已有项目与诊断原因，并显示简短的本地化重试提示；已被另一设备处理或已过期的决定不再计为本次提交成功（[#2500](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2500)）。
- 远程活动中无法读取的项目会显示重试提示，有效项目仍可操作；打开会话失败后，重试仍指向同一 Host（[#2500](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2500)）。
- 活动中心和会话面板切换远程 Host 或频道时会清空原会话尚未发送的草稿，避免误发给新来源（[#2500](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2500)）。
