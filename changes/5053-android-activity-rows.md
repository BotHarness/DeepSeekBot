## en

- On Android at large system text sizes, Activity rows show the full Bot or group name on up to two lines, put the time on its own line, and give the preview two lines, as Chats rows already do ([#5053](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5053)).

## zh

- 在 Android 上使用大号系统文字时，动态列表的每一行会把 Bot 或群组名称完整显示为最多两行，时间单独占一行，预览显示两行，与对话列表一致（[#5053](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5053)）。
