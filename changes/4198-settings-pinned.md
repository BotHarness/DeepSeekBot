## en

- Settings opens on Pinned, a page of cards for the settings you use most. Select the star beside any page title to pin or unpin it; pins are saved on the Host, shared by every client of that Host, and follow the chosen environment, and `deepseekbot settings-set botharness-client` can set them as `settingsPins` ([#4198](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4198)).

## zh

- 设置默认打开「常用」页，用卡片列出最常用的设置。点任意设置页标题旁的星标即可固定或取消固定；固定项保存在 Host 上，连接同一 Host 的客户端共用，并跟随当前选中的环境，也可以用 `deepseekbot settings-set botharness-client` 设置 `settingsPins`（[#4198](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4198)）。
