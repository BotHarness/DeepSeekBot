## en

- Connect two computers with one explicitly approved reciprocal relationship, then read and message each other's Channels using separate client credentials. Disconnect revokes relationship-derived access while preserving independently paired devices. Relationship management remains current-Owner-only and respects Team/private-Bot boundaries and Owner transfer ([#4110](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4110)).

## zh

- 两台电脑核对身份并批准一次双向关系后，可通过各自独立的客户端凭据查看对方频道、发送消息；断开关系撤销由此签发的权限，保留独立配对设备。关系管理仅属于当前 Owner，并遵守 Team、私有 Bot 和 Owner 转移边界（[#4110](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4110)）。
