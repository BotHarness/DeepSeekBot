## en

- A message's delivery status now marks an @-mentioned Bot whose Inbox never received the message as Not received. Click a Bot in the status card to open its Inbox history, with the item for that message and any note on why it was ignored shown first ([#2871](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2871)).

## zh

- 消息的收件状态现在会把被 @ 却没有进入收件箱的 Bot 标为“未收到”。在状态卡片里点击某个 Bot，即可查看它的收件记录，这条消息对应的条目和忽略原因会排在最前面（[#2871](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2871)）。
