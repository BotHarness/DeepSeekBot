## en

- Bot Marketplace cards now show banners with flat, borderless surfaces and primary Import buttons; search, upload and sort controls share one height. Browsing defaults to most stars, remembers your sort on this device, and breaks star ties by last update then creation time ([#2568](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2568)).

## zh

- Bot 市场卡片显示顶部横幅，采用扁平无描边样式和主色导入按钮，搜索框、上传和排序控件统一高度。默认按最多 Star 排序，在当前设备记住排序选择；Star 相同时依次按最后更新和创建时间排序（[#2568](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2568)）。
