## en

- While you share your camera or screen on a call and stay silent, every 16 keyframes are saved as a frame record in the DM without waking the Bot, and your next sentence tells the Bot which records came before, so it can follow a film or a long demo in order. Turning off frame saving keeps grids out of the DM while the Bot still sees them during the call. `deepseekbot call-simulate --no-save` drives the switch ([#3494](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3494)).
