## en

- iPhone calls can turn the camera on. A self-preview with a switch-camera button replaces the Bot's picture, and a counter shows how many keyframes went to the Bot. When the app goes to the background the camera pauses, the call keeps going with voice, and the DM notes both the pause and the return ([#3497](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3497)).
- If a Bot's model cannot read images, the call no longer fails its turn: the Bot is told a camera or screen is shared that it cannot see with this model ([#3497](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3497)).

## zh

- iPhone 通话可以打开摄像头。自拍预览（带切换前后摄像头的按钮）会替换 Bot 的头像，并显示已发给 Bot 的关键帧数。App 切到后台时摄像头暂停，通话继续保持语音，DM 里会记录暂停和恢复（[#3497](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3497)）。
- 如果 Bot 的模型不能读图片，通话不再让这一轮失败：Bot 会被告知对方正在共享摄像头或屏幕，但它用当前模型看不到（[#3497](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3497)）。
