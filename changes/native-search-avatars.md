## en

- On Android, Chats search results now show each conversation's own avatar as the same rounded square as the chat list, instead of a coloured circle with its first letter. Activity rows also use rounded-square avatars, and an unread group conversation shows its group picture instead of the last Bot who wrote there ([#2953](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2953)).

## zh

- Android 上聊天列表搜索结果现在显示每个会话自己的头像，并与列表一样是圆角矩形，不再是带首字母的彩色圆。动态页的头像也改为圆角矩形，群聊未读显示群头像，而不是最后发言的 Bot（[#2953](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2953)）。
