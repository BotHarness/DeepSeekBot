## en

- Android Computer uses a black canvas and dark controls in either app theme, with a leading Back button, a separate Host environment capsule with connection and control icons, and grouped native Material actions. The keyboard button shows whether input is open. A short blue or orange ring marks acknowledged left or right clicks at the pointer position. ([#2559](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2559))

## zh

- Android Computer 在两种应用主题下都使用纯黑画布和深色控件，左侧返回按钮与 显示连接及控制图标的 Host 环境胶囊分开，底部操作使用分组的原生 Material 按钮，键盘按钮显示输入是否开启；左右键点击确认后，分别在鼠标位置显示短暂的蓝色或橙色波纹。([#2559](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2559))
