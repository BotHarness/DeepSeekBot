## en

- You can now finish an App connection sign-in such as PostHog on your phone or any other device. When the Host has a public https address (Tobs Connect, a Tailscale HTTPS address, or your own https address), the sign-in returns there instead of to this computer. Sign-in links now stay valid for 60 minutes instead of 15 ([#5333](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5333)).

## zh

- PostHog 等应用连接的登录现在可以在手机或任何设备上完成。Host 有公网 https 地址（Tobs Connect、Tailscale HTTPS 地址，或你自己的 https 地址）时，登录完成后回到这个地址，而不是只能回到这台电脑。登录链接的有效期从 15 分钟放宽到 60 分钟（[#5333](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5333)）。
