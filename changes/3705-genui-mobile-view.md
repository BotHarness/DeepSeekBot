## en

- The mobile app shows rich GenUI documents from Bots inside the conversation, beside their readable summary, in an isolated view-only frame that follows the app's theme and text size. Saving, resetting and continuing a document still happen on the computer ([#3705](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3705)).

- On the phone, GenUI documents now restore and save your filters, selections and other declared view choices through the same private view as the computer, with reset and conflict recovery when another device changed it first ([#3705](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3705)).

## zh

- 手机 App 现在会在对话中显示 Bot 发送的富 GenUI 文档，与可读摘要并列，放在隔离的只读框中，并跟随 App 的主题和字号。保存、重置和续问文档仍需在电脑上完成（[#3705](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3705)）。
- 手机上的 GenUI 文档现在会通过与电脑相同的私有视图恢复并保存筛选、选择等已声明的视图状态；可以重置，其他设备先改动时也能处理冲突（[#3705](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3705)）。
