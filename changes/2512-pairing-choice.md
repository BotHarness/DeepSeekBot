## en

- On iOS and Android, pairing now starts with a choice: sign in with a DeepSeekBot account to pair the computers on it, or use Tailscale to scan a pairing code or paste a link. A phone with no computer opens this sheet on launch, and cloud Channels stay hidden unless their feature flag is on ([#2512](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2512)).

## zh

- iOS 和 Android 的配对现在先让你选方式：登录 DeepSeekBot 账号来配对账号下的电脑，或者用 Tailscale 扫描配对二维码、粘贴链接。还没有电脑时，打开 App 会自动弹出这个配对 sheet；云端频道在 feature flag 打开前保持隐藏（[#2512](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2512)）。
