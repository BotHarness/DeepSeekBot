## en

- A call with a Bot on iPhone now keeps going when you lock the phone or switch to another app. The Lock Screen and Dynamic Island show the call with its time, what the Bot is doing, and Mute and Hang Up buttons. A new Audio button on the call screen switches between Speaker, iPhone (at your ear) and Media Audio, which plays at the media volume and in full quality on Bluetooth headphones; the phone remembers your choice. A phone call or another app pauses the call instead of ending it ([#3422](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3422)).

## zh

- 在 iPhone 上和 Bot 通话时，锁屏或切到其他 App 后通话会继续。锁屏和灵动岛会显示通话时长、Bot 正在做什么，以及静音和挂断按钮。通话页新增“音频”按钮，可以在扬声器、听筒和媒体音频之间切换；媒体音频使用媒体音量，蓝牙耳机保持高音质。手机会记住你的选择。来电或其他 App 占用音频时，通话会暂停而不是挂断（[#3422](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3422)）。
