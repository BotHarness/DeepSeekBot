## en

- The phone sorts the Sessions list by last Human turn, creation or last activity from the copy on the device, and shows title matches as soon as you type while the Host adds matches in earlier messages. A renamed Bot shows its new name without a full reload, and a Bot made private disappears from members' lists right away ([#5356](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5356)).

## zh

- 手机按本机保存的会话列表排序（最近 Human 消息、创建时间或最近活动），输入搜索词时会立刻显示标题匹配的会话，Host 随后补上历史消息中的匹配。Bot 改名后无需重新加载整个列表即可显示新名称；Bot 设为私有后，会立即从团队成员的列表中消失（[#5356](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5356)）。
