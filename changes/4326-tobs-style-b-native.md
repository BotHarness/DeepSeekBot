## en

- Appearance settings gain a Style choice. Next to the default Quiet style there is now Native: settings rows sit in white grouped cards with a hairline edge on a grey backdrop, the selected navigation item takes the accent colour with white text, and controls are more compact. The style is saved per device and can also be set with `deepseekbot appearance-set` ([#4326](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4326)).

## zh

- 界面设置新增「风格」选项。除了默认的「简洁」，现在可以选「原生」：设置行放在灰色背景上带细边的白色分组卡片里，选中的导航项用强调色底配白字，控件更紧凑。风格按设备保存，也可以用 `deepseekbot appearance-set` 设置（[#4326](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4326)）。
