## en

- During a desktop call, the microphone button in the call bar ripples while you speak, with the same effect as voice input in the message box, so you can see the call is hearing you. The left control rail also shows the Bot you are calling, with a phone badge on its avatar; click it to return to that chat from any other conversation.

## zh

- 桌面端通话时，说话会让通话栏里的麦克风按钮出现波纹，效果和消息输入框语音输入一样，一眼就能看出通话正在收音。左侧控制栏也会显示正在通话的 Bot，头像右下角带通话标记；在别的聊天里点它就能回到这个通话。
