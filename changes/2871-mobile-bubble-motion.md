## en

- On mobile, new messages, including your own, now scale into the chat instead of appearing at once, as do the reading indicator and reaction chips, and single-line messages render as fully rounded pills. Replies now show complete by default; streaming text stays available under message display settings ([#2871](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2871)).

## zh

- 手机端新消息（包括自己发的）、正在阅读提示和 reaction chip 出现时都会有缩放入场动画，单行消息显示为全圆角的胶囊形状。回复默认改为完整后再显示，流式显示仍可在消息显示设置中开启（[#2871](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2871)）。
