## en

- Added app-wide themes: choose from four color presets per light/dark mode, set a photo or video wallpaper that shows behind the whole app, and turn on translucent surfaces. Changes apply immediately across the app, and theme choices are saved on this phone ([#4](https://github.com/skyc10/DeepSeekBot/issues/4)).
