## en

- On the phone, linked Sessions appear inside the Bot message. The Session detail header uses the chat layout and offers Stop, Model choice, and Archive or Unarchive for eligible Assignments ([#4931](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4931)).

## zh

- 手机上的关联会话显示在 Bot 消息气泡内。会话详情沿用聊天页的顶部布局，菜单可停止会话、选择模型，或对符合条件的事项归档和取消归档（[#4931](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4931)）。
