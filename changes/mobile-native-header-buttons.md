## en

- On the phone's Chats list, Search and Settings are now native buttons: Liquid Glass on iOS 26 and later, and Material 3 icon buttons on Android. Earlier iOS keeps the current round buttons.

## zh

- 手机端聊天列表右上角的搜索和设置改用系统原生按钮：iOS 26 及以上为液态玻璃（Liquid Glass）按钮，Android 为 Material 3 图标按钮；更早的 iOS 保持原来的圆形按钮。
