## en

- Mostly internal: the remaining plugin stylesheets, including the Computer and Browser settings, now read their spacing, type, radius and colour from the shared design tokens, and unused legacy styles and per-feature token tables are gone. A few gaps, paddings and corners move by up to 2px to sit on the shared scale, small 10–11px labels become 12px, and 15px and 18px titles step to 14px and 16px; measured native rows, the rename field and the composer keep their exact geometry ([#1603](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1603)).

## zh

- 以内部整理为主：其余插件样式表（包括 Computer 和 Browser 设置）现在统一从共享设计令牌取间距、字号、圆角和颜色，不再使用的旧样式和各功能自带的令牌表已移除。少量间距、内边距和圆角调整至多 2px 以对齐共享刻度，10–11px 的小号文字调到 12px，15px 和 18px 的标题分别调到 14px 和 16px；按原生测量的行高、重命名输入框和输入框保持原有尺寸（[#1603](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/1603)）。
