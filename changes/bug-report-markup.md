## en

- The Report a problem dialog can take a screenshot of a window or tab and mark it up before it is packed: draw a box or a line, or cover anything private. Image attachments get the same markup button, and the marked-up copy replaces the original (PR_LINK).

## zh

- 报告问题窗口可以直接截取窗口或标签页并标注后再打包：画框、画线，或遮住不想给别人看的内容。已添加的图片附件也能点“标注”，标注后的图片会替换原图（PR_LINK）。
