## en

- On iPhone at large text sizes, list chevrons, row icons and model logos now grow with their labels. Typed text in single-line fields such as search and the pairing link now grows too. At the largest sizes, environment and account rows put their picture above the name, so long names no longer break mid-word. The Settings account picture now shows a visible circle ([#5053](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5053)).

## zh

- 在 iPhone 上使用大号文字时，列表里的箭头、行图标和模型图标会跟着文字一起变大；搜索框、配对链接等单行输入框里输入的文字也会变大。在最大的几档字号下，环境和账号行会把图标放到名字上方，长名字不再从单词中间断开。设置里的账号头像现在有可见的圆形底色（[#5053](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5053)）。
