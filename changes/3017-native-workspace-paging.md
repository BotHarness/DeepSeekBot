## en

- On iPhone, swipe right from Chats to Workspaces and left back through native retained pages. Chats and its tab bar move together; looking at Workspaces preserves the environment, search and list position instead of rebuilding Chats ([#3017](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3017)).

- Chat profiles use the same native retained-page presentation on iPhone, with the profile on the right and each page owning its native navigation toolbar; profile child routes retain their explicit Computer identity ([#3109](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3109)).

## zh

- iPhone 上通过原生常驻页面，右滑打开工作区、左滑返回聊天列表。聊天列表和底部导航栏整体移动；仅查看工作区会保留当前环境、搜索和滚动位置，不再重新创建聊天列表（[#3017](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3017)）。

- iPhone 聊天资料页也采用同样的原生常驻分页，资料页位于右侧，各页拥有自己的原生导航工具栏；资料子页面始终保留明确的电脑来源（[#3109](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3109)）。
