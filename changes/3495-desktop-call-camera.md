## en

- Desktop and Web calls can turn the camera on. A small self-preview shows under the call bar and flashes each time a keyframe goes to the Bot, and a switch there stops saving frames to the chat. If the camera is blocked, the call keeps going and the preview explains how to allow it ([#3495](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3495)).

## zh

- 桌面和 Web 通话可以打开摄像头。通话栏下方会出现小的自拍预览，每发给 Bot 一个关键帧，预览边缘就闪一下；预览里的开关可以停止把画面存到聊天。摄像头被禁用时通话照常进行，预览里会说明怎么开启（[#3495](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3495)）。
