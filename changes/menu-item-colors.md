## en

- Action and context menus (for example a computer's ··· menu on the iOS workspace panel) no longer show blue icons or icons in a different colour from their labels: ordinary items are black in light mode and white in dark mode, and destructive items such as Unpair or Delete are red ([#PR](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/PR)).

## zh

- 操作菜单和长按菜单（例如 iOS 工作区面板里电脑的 ··· 菜单）不再出现蓝色图标，图标和文字颜色也不再不一致：普通选项在浅色模式下是黑色、深色模式下是白色，取消配对、删除等危险选项为红色（[#PR](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/PR)）。
