## en

- Manage the selected remote environment’s devices, individual routes and account sign-out from its settings, with explicit disconnection or verified route handover and persistent protection against repeating an uncertain write. CLI private stops require `--connection-route-id`; self-disconnecting writes require `--handover` or `--confirm-disconnect` ([#4120](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4120)).

## zh

- 在所选远程环境的设置中管理设备、单条路由和账号退出；执行前明确确认断开或验证并切换路由，操作结果未知时持久保留保护，避免重复提交。CLI 停止私有路由须指定 `--connection-route-id`；可能自行断开的写入须指定 `--handover` 或 `--confirm-disconnect`（[#4120](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4120)）。
