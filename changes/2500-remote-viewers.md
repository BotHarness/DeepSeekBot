## en

- Open a Remote Host Bot's Browser or Computer viewer from its conversation, pause the Bot to send input, and release control when the viewer closes. The viewers reuse the paired-device frame and input routes ([#2500](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2500)).
