## en

- The desktop Bot mode workspace no longer has a Home panel; Tob mode replaces it. The Tob mode switch takes Home's rail position and shortcut (⌘⌥⇧H / Ctrl+Alt+Shift+H, or Ctrl+Alt+Shift+O on Linux Desktop; custom bindings keep working), saved Simple and Advanced layouts drop Home and keep every other panel where it was (the previous layout is kept as a local backup), and the last onboarding step finishes when you turn Tob mode on. Activity Center keeps the cross-Bot overview, and the default model stays in Bot settings › Models & execution ([#3089](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3089)).

## zh

- 桌面 Bot 模式工作区不再提供「首页」面板，由托布模式取代。托布模式开关接替首页在导轨上的位置和快捷键（⌘⌥⇧H / Ctrl+Alt+Shift+H，Linux 桌面端为 Ctrl+Alt+Shift+O；自定义快捷键继续有效）；已保存的简单和高级布局会移除首页，其他面板保持原位（旧布局保留为本地备份）；新手引导的最后一步改为打开托布模式即完成。跨 Bot 概览继续由活动中心提供，默认模型仍在 Bot 设置 › 模型与运行中设置（[#3089](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/3089)）。
