## en

- When a Bot starts Assignments, sends exactly one message in your DM during that turn and forgets to link them, the Host now links those Assignments to that message at the end of the turn ([#4550](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4550)).

## zh

- Bot 在一个回合里启动了 Assignment、在你的私聊里只发了一条消息却忘了绑定时，Host 会在回合结束时把这些 Assignment 绑定到那条消息上（[#4550](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4550)）。
