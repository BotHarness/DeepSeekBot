## en

- Android Chats and Activity now hide their headers with navigation while scrolling down and restore them while scrolling up, keeping controls visible during search and with TalkBack ([#2686](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2686)).

- Android 聊天与动态页面向下滚动时同步收起顶部和底部导航，向上滚动时恢复；搜索和 TalkBack 开启时保持控件可见（[#2686](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2686)）。
