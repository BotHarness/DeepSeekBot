## en

- The Owner can let any of their Bots manage the App. `deepseekbot bot-grant <bot> manage-app|manage-bots on|off` turns "Manage the App" and "Manage other Bots" on or off, and `deepseekbot bot-grants` lists the holders. Tob has both by default and is now an ordinary Bot that can be archived, deleted and shared. Writes follow the Bot's Approval Template; deletion, other Bots' Memory, Profile Backup restore, credentials and pairing always ask ([#5193](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5193)).

## zh

- Owner 可以让自己的任意 Bot 管理 App。`deepseekbot bot-grant <bot> manage-app|manage-bots on|off` 打开或关闭“管理 App”和“管理其他 Bot”，`deepseekbot bot-grants` 列出持有者。Tob 默认两项都开，现在是普通 Bot，可以归档、删除和分享。写操作跟随该 Bot 的审批模板；删除、其他 Bot 的 Memory、恢复 Profile Backup、凭据和配对总是需要确认（[#5193](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5193)）。
