## en

- Motion outside the chat now follows the same feel as chat bubbles: the Window Companion's speech bubble and pending question grow in from 92% with a quick settle instead of popping from half size and overshooting, and its answer options rise in without a bounce. Switching model mode and dismissing a handled approval in the Activity inbox use the same quick ease-out, and dismissal is a little faster. Marketplace cards lift on hover only with a mouse, never on touch. With **Reduce motion** set in the app, the sessions list no longer slides rows when they reorder, header controls fade without sliding, and the memory diff and release note chevrons turn without animating. On mobile, the Bot profile and its tab switches open with the same ease-out as chat, a swiped-away photo leaves at once instead of starting slowly, and the undo bar fades in and out faster.

## zh

- 聊天以外的动效与聊天气泡统一手感：桌宠的对话气泡和待回答问题从 92% 大小快速展开，不再从一半大小弹出并回弹，选项也去掉了回弹；切换模型模式、在动态里处理掉的审批卡片离场使用同一条快速缓出曲线，离场更快一点。插件市场卡片只在鼠标悬停时轻微抬起，触屏不再误触发。在应用里开启**减少动效**后，会话列表重新排序时不再滑动，顶部控件只淡入淡出不再位移，记忆差异和更新说明的展开箭头直接切换。手机端 Bot 资料页打开和切换标签使用与聊天相同的缓出曲线，看图时滑走的图片立即离开而不是先慢后快，撤销提示条的出现和消失也更快。
