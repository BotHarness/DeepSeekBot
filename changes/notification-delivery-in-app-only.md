## en

- Notification settings on the phone now have a Delivery choice for each computer: Push, In app only, or Off. In app only stops pushes to that phone but keeps its badge up to date, and shows banners only while the app is open. The CLI sets it with `deepseekbot device-notifications --delivery push|in-app` (#PR).

## zh

- 手机的通知设置新增“送达方式”，可为每台电脑选择：推送、仅 App 内或关闭。仅 App 内不再推送到这台手机，但角标照常更新，只在打开 App 时显示横幅。CLI 用 `deepseekbot device-notifications --delivery push|in-app` 设置（#PR）。
