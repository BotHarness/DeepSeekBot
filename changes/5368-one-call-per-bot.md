## en

- You can call several Bots at once: each Bot takes one call, and a computer runs up to three. Calling a Bot that is already on a call says so, and a fourth call asks you to end one first ([#5368](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5368), [Voice and call decisions §4](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/blob/main/docs/decisions/calls.md#adr-0281)).

## zh

- 现在可以同时和多个 Bot 通话：每个 Bot 同时接一通，一台电脑最多三通。打给正在通话的 Bot 会提示它在通话中，第四通会提示先结束一通（[#5368](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/5368)、[语音和通话决定 §4](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/blob/main/docs/decisions/calls.md#adr-0281)）。
