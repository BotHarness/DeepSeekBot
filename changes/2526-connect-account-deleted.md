## en

- A Host signed in to DeepSeekBot Connect now notices within about a minute when its account is deleted or its session is revoked: it stops the tunnel, forgets the session and shows **Sign in again** in Settings → Devices and in `deepseekbot connect-status`, instead of staying Connected ([#2526](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2526)).

## zh

- 登录 DeepSeekBot Connect 的 Host 现在会在大约一分钟内发现账号已删除或会话已撤销：停掉隧道、清除会话，并在设置 → 设备和 `deepseekbot connect-status` 中提示重新登录，不再一直显示已连接（[#2526](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2526)）。
