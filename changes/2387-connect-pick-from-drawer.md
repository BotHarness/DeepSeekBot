- **Pair account computers from the side drawer**: when the phone is signed in to DeepSeekBot Connect, the Computers drawer lists the account's computers that are not paired yet, and tapping one pairs it ([#2387](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2387)).
