## en

- **Pair account computers from the side drawer**: when the phone is signed in to DeepSeekBot Connect, the Computers drawer lists the account's computers that are not paired yet, and tapping one pairs it. On the pairing sheet, the account's computers now come before the QR scanner ([#2387](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2387)).

## zh

- **从左侧抽屉直接配对账号里的电脑**：手机登录 DeepSeekBot Connect 后，左侧「电脑」抽屉会列出账号里还没配对的电脑，点一下就能配对。配对页上，账号里的电脑也移到了扫码框上面（[#2387](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2387)）。
