## en

- On mobile, your messages that reach Bots now show a small delivery status pie, including Bots you @-mentioned that never received the message. Tap it to see each Bot's state, then tap a Bot to open its Inbox history with the item for that message tagged ([#2871](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2871)).

## zh

- 手机端发给 Bot 的消息旁现在会显示投递状态小饼图，被 @ 却没收到消息的 Bot 也会标出来。点开可查看每个 Bot 的状态，再点某个 Bot 即可查看它的收件记录，这条消息对应的条目会带标签（[#2871](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2871)）。
