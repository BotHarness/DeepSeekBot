## en

- Chats rows give more room to conversation details by removing the persistent overflow button. Swipe for Pin, Add to section and Mute; long press for the full menu, including Mark as read, with the existing preview on iOS. Screen-reader actions remain available, and the header, avatars and swipe rail share a horizontal gutter ([#2588](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2588)).

## zh

- 聊天行移除常驻更多按钮，为会话内容留出空间。滑动可置顶、添加到分区和静音；长按打开包含标为已读的完整菜单，iOS 保留原有预览。屏幕阅读器操作继续可用，标题栏、头像和滑动操作栏采用统一水平留白（[#2588](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2588)）。
