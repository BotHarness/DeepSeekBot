## en

- Mobile Connect groups account actions and computers into native sections, shows offline and already-added computers, and adds selected computers one at a time with independent fingerprint checks. New connections keep their computer names; rename them later in Settings ([#2581](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2581)).

## zh

- 手机 Connect 使用原生分组展示账户操作和电脑，标明离线及已添加状态，支持多选后逐台核对指纹并添加。新增连接默认保留电脑名称，可稍后在设置中重命名（[#2581](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2581)）。
