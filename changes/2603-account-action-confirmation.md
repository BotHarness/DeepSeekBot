## en

- Sign out and Delete account now require confirmation explaining their different effects. Confirmed actions remove this phone’s connections recorded as belonging to that account, preserve manual and older unassigned pairings, and retain recovery state when cleanup or deletion is incomplete, with an explicitly confirmed phone-only recovery when the remote result is unknown ([#2603](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2603)).

## zh

- 退出登录和删除账号均需二次确认，并说明各自影响。确认后移除此手机中已记录归属该账号的连接，保留手动和未记录归属的旧配对；清理或删除未完成时保留恢复状态，远端结果未知时可再次确认仅清理此手机（[#2603](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/2603)）。
