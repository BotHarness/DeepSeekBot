## en

- On iOS 26 the phone's Computers / Cloud sidebar floats on Liquid Glass with a native pull-down menu for each computer, and the Chats search field sits just above the bottom navigation where the thumb reaches it. Form fields share one look, item menus use iOS action sheets, and unpairing from Settings, removing folder access, resetting a computer's look, revoking cloud access and signing out now ask first. In chats, messages blur and fade softly under the header and the composer, and the header buttons and title use Liquid Glass ([#2333](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2333)).

## zh

- 在 iOS 26 上，手机端的「电脑 / 云端」侧边栏改为悬浮的液态玻璃面板，每台电脑的 ⋯ 打开原生下拉菜单；聊天搜索框移到底部导航上方，单手更容易点到。表单输入框统一样式，条目菜单改用 iOS 操作表；在设置中取消配对、移除文件夹授权、重置电脑外观、撤销云端访问和退出登录前都会先确认。聊天页里，消息在顶部标题栏和底部输入框下方柔和地模糊并渐隐，标题栏按钮和标题胶囊也改用液态玻璃（[#2333](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/pull/2333)）。
