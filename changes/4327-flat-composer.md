## en

- The message box and the jump-to-latest button are flat: no shadow or outline, only a fill that sets them apart. The message box has rounder corners, round attach, voice and send buttons, and still moves the text up to the full width when it wraps. The message list now runs under the floating message box and fades out at the bottom like it does at the top. With unread messages, the jump button turns the accent colour and shows the count (up to 99+) in place of the arrow ([#4327](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4327)).

## zh

- 消息输入框和「返回最新消息」按钮改成扁平样式：没有阴影和描边，只用底色区分。输入框圆角更大，附件、语音和发送按钮是正圆；文字换行时仍会移到上方、占满整行宽度。消息列表铺到底部，输入框悬浮在列表上，底部和顶部一样渐隐。有未读消息时，返回按钮变成主色并直接显示数字（最多 99+）替代箭头（[#4327](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4327)）。
