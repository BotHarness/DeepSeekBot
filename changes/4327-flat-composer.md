## en

- The message box and the jump-to-latest button are flat: no shadow or outline, only a fill that sets them apart. With unread messages, the jump button shows the count itself (up to 99+) in place of the arrow, and its corners match the message box ([#4327](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4327)).

## zh

- 消息输入框和「返回最新消息」按钮改成扁平样式：没有阴影和描边，只用底色区分。有未读消息时，按钮直接显示数字（最多 99+）替代箭头，圆角和输入框一致（[#4327](https://github.com/BotHarness/DeepSeekBot/issues/4327)）。
