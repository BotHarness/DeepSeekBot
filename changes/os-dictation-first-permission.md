## en

- On iOS, the first dictation with the phone's own speech recognition no longer fails with "Voice input stopped" right after you allow speech recognition; it now starts listening once the permission prompt closes.

## zh

- iOS 上第一次使用手机自带语音识别听写时，刚允许语音识别权限后不再立刻提示“语音输入中途停止了”；现在会等授权弹窗关闭后再开始听写。
