# Bot calls: phones and the Mac or Windows desktop

A paired iPhone or Android phone, or the Mac or Windows desktop Client (`desktop.bot-call`), calls a Bot. The Host's media helper carries WebRTC audio, the Host call owner cuts sentences with its built-in VAD, transcribes them through the Bot's ASR Provider and sends them to the DM, and the Bot's committed voice replies play back in the call. See [ADR-0281](../../../../docs/adr/0281-host-owned-bot-calls.md), [ADR-0284](../../../../docs/adr/0284-android-bot-calls-are-foreground-only.md) (Android, foreground only) and the [CLI contract](../../../../docs/bot-cli.md#bot-calls-and-speech-recognition-asr-providers).

## Sub-features

- `readiness`: the chat header's call button asks the Host first and names a missing ASR or TTS Provider, media helper or unsupported Host. `deepseekbot call-readiness <bot>` returns the same `missing` list.
- `turns`: speech becomes the Human's DM message; coughs and knocks are `noise-discarded`. The Bot replies with `voice: true` and its audio plays in the call, then stays as a voice bar with text after hang-up.
- `long-task`: the Bot first says a short line such as "Let me check", dispatches an Assignment, and speaks the result when it returns.
- `text-only`: after "send me text", replies carry no voice.
- `barge-in`: while the Bot speaks, about 200 ms of speech ducks its voice to 30% (`barge-suspected`), about 500 ms or two non-filler words stop it (`barge-in`, `reply-stopped` reason `interrupted`) and the Human's sentence goes to the DM. Coughs, knocks, short noise, a murmured "嗯" and the Bot's own echo end in `barge-dismissed` or nothing. The interrupted reply's voice bar holds only the heard sentences.
- `busy`: while the Orchestrator is on an earlier turn, the phone shows "It's busy. It will hear you in its next turn." and nothing is spoken.
- `microphone`: a refused microphone offers Allow microphone; a blocked one offers Open Settings and the call starts when the app returns with it allowed.
- `lifecycle`: mute, hang up, background and lock on Android, 8 s network loss (`connection-lost`) and device revoke end or pause the call cleanly; committed messages remain. Connect routes are refused with `connect-carrier-required`.
- `background` (#3422, [ADR-0285](../../../../docs/adr/0285-bot-calls-keep-running-in-the-background.md)): locking the iPhone or switching apps keeps the call; the Host shows no `ended` event. The `TobsCall` Live Activity shows the Bot, the call timer, the status and Mute and Hang Up buttons on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island; Hang Up there ends the call as `hangup`. A phone call or another app pausing the audio shows the paused hint and resumes after.
- `audio-route`: the call screen's Audio pull-down (`call-audio`) has a Microphone section (iPhone, the headset by name) and an Output section (iPhone, the connected headset by name, Speaker); the ticks follow the devices actually in use. Headphones default to their own microphone (HFP); picking the iPhone microphone is music mode (A2DP, other apps keep playing). Switching never drops the call; a headset connecting mid-call takes the audio; Speaker and iPhone work while headphones are connected. The phone remembers the microphone chosen for headphones.
- `desktop`: in a Bot DM the chat header shows a phone button (`Call <Bot>`). During a call it becomes the call bar `Call with <Bot>`: status, call time, `Mute`/`Unmute` and `Hang up`. The chat keeps scrolling under it and voice replies appear in the timeline as they come. The browser microphone runs with echo cancellation. A second desktop window or device gets "Another device or window is on a call with this computer." and the first call goes on.
- `records` (#3483): a call that connects leaves a call bubble with its start time in the DM, and one with its length when it ends (desktop timeline and mobile chat, and the Chats preview). The Bot reads both as `voiceCallRecord` system messages in `channel_read`; the first typed message after a call carries a `[Last voice call …]` line with the call's `from`/`to`. `call-simulate` leaves the same two notices.
- `media`: the Host helper offers one Opus audio section with no STUN/TURN, returns 16 kHz PCM frames and plays reply files.

## How to get to it (user POV)

On a Mac or Windows Host, add an ASR and a TTS Provider in Settings › Voice or with `deepseekbot voice-provider-add`, bind both to a Bot (`voice-set`, `voice-asr-set`), and open Local Computer once so the media helper is installed. Pair the rebuilt iOS or Android development app on the same Wi-Fi or Tailscale, open the Bot's DM and tap the phone button in the header.

## Driving it with verify-app

1. Host only, no phone: `deepseekbot call-simulate <bot> --file /abs/question.wav` (or `--script` with a cough between two sentences) against a Host with the latest DeepSeek model. Check `heard`, `replies`, `firstAudioMs` and that the cough appears only as `noise-discarded`.
2. Barge-in without a phone: a `--script` step `{"after": "playing", "at": 1500, "file": "/abs/cut.wav"}` speaks over the Bot; check `bargeIns`, the second `heard` entry and that the first reply's `voice-ready` bytes equal the sentences that played. Self-echo needs a real phone speaker.
3. Media helper without Electron: `node scripts/e2e-call-media.mjs` loads the helper's renderer in Chromium, connects a phone page and checks Opus DTX (a silent side sends ≤ 10 packets/s), uplink PCM, downlink playback, the barge-in duck and restore, back-to-back clips, failed playback and close.
4. Desktop without a Mac or Windows Host: `node scripts/e2e-desktop-call.mjs [--scenario question,long-task|/abs/timeline.json] [--json]` starts Chromium with `--use-file-for-fake-audio-capture`, runs the Client's real call store and browser media against the helper renderer and the real Host call owner, and checks the heard sentence, the discarded cough, the replies heard on the desktop and that a second window is refused. The timeline format is the same as `call-simulate --script`. On macOS, and with `--wav-mic`, the script feeds the WAV through a page-level `getUserMedia` stand-in, because Chromium's fake capture still triggers the system microphone check there. On a real Mac or Windows Host, open the Bot DM in the desktop app and press `Call <Bot>`.
5. iPhone: use a `.dev` development build. Open `deepseekbot-dev://call/<bot>?script=<url-of-timeline.json>` where the timeline is `[{"at": 0, "url": "https://…/question.wav"}]`; the build replaces the microphone with those files, records the downlink and writes a timing report path on the call screen (`call-test-report`). Use `chat-call-button`, `call-mute`, `call-hang-up`, `call-status`, `call-duration` and `call-hint`.
6. Android: the same `.dev` link works on the Windows/Android lane (`adb shell am start -a android.intent.action.VIEW -d "deepseekbot-dev://call/<bot>?script=<url>"`). The report path and the downlink WAV are in the app cache (`adb shell run-as ai.botharness.deepseekbot.dev ls cache`). Also check: deny the microphone once (Allow microphone asks again), deny twice (Open Settings, and the call starts on return once allowed), lock the phone mid-call (call ends as hang-up), and echo on the loudspeaker with a real voice on a physical phone.
7. Background (real iPhone only): with a `script` call running, lock the phone before the next injected sentence; the downlink report still shows `downlink-start` and the DM gets the reply. Check the Lock Screen Live Activity, press Hang Up there, and confirm the Host logs `call-ended` with `hangup`. With Bluetooth headphones, pick the iPhone microphone and confirm music-quality sound and that YouTube or Apple Music keeps playing under the Bot; pick the headset microphone and confirm call quality, then play YouTube and confirm the call shows paused and resumes. Pick Speaker and iPhone and confirm the sound moves there. Start a FaceTime or phone call to the iPhone during a Bot call and confirm the paused hint, then the resume.
8. Minimize (#3468, iPhone and Android): during a `script` call press `call-minimize` (Android: also system Back), open another chat and send a message; the `call-bubble` shows the ring, `call-bubble-status` keeps counting and the Host logs no `call-ended`. Drag the bubble to the other side, tap it, and confirm the same call returns without dialing again (no second `call-started`). Hang up from the Host side while minimized and check the bubble reads "Call ended".
9. Performance (#3481): during a real call run `deepseekbot call-status` on the Host and read `perf` (packets/s, kbps, loss, RTT, helper and Host CPU per direction, sampled every 5 s); after hang-up read the `call-media-summary` line in `logs.db`. A silent phone should show `uplinkPps` near 2–6 (Opus DTX), not 50. Compare before/after runs with the same timeline. On the phone, a `.dev` build's call report adds a `native` `perf` event every 5 s with cumulative `packetsSent`/`bytesSent` (phone uplink), `packetsReceived`/`bytesReceived`/`packetsLost`/`concealedSamples` (downlink), `jitterMs`, `rttMs`, process `cpuMs`, and the thermal state (iOS `thermal` 0–3 and `lowPower`; Android `thermal` 0–6, `batteryPct` and `batteryCurrentUa`). Diff consecutive samples for rates and CPU %.
10. Capture the call screen in light and dark, at default and largest text, connecting, listening, speaking, busy and ended. Record the first-sentence audio time from `firstAudioMs` or the report.

## Gotchas

- Calls never use Connect; a phone on cellular without Tailscale gets the "same Wi-Fi or Tailscale" explanation.
- Calls are full duplex (V4): speech during the Bot's reply is judged as a barge-in. For 500 ms after each clip starts and 300 ms after it ends the VAD needs 14 dB more level, so the start of a quiet reply over the Bot may be missed.
- One call per Host; a second device gets `call-active`.
- Nothing is recorded on the Host. Only the `.dev` test build records the downlink, on the phone.

## Android output menu (#3632)

On the assigned physical Android 12+ phone, connect a foreground Bot call and open `call-audio`. Choose `call-audio-output-receiver`, then `call-audio-output-speaker`, both unmuted and muted. Require the same live call ID and confirm the native communication device, not only the selected row. Hang up and verify restoration of the pre-call audio mode. Repeat with an available communication headset and device removal; verify unavailable choices disappear. Record any unavailable hardware explicitly. Exercise native Back dismissal and default/intermediate/maximum text in light/dark. Independent microphone selection and saved preferences remain in #3632. iOS `audio-route` above remains a separate journey.

## Android playback mode (#3632)

On the assigned physical Android 13+ phone, use the native audio menu to switch from Call mode to Media mode and back, both muted and recording. Keep the same live call ID. Confirm the active AudioTrack uses USAGE_MEDIA with MODE_NORMAL, then USAGE_VOICE_COMMUNICATION with MODE_IN_COMMUNICATION. Returning to Call mode restores the previous available communication output. Check an actual Bot reply in each mode, headset removal and hang-up restoration separately; routing-state evidence alone does not prove sound quality. Android 12 retains the output menu without mode choices. The pinned M144 public JavaAudioDeviceModule input setter dereferences a null device; clearing the input preference uses its null-safe package-level WebRtcAudioRecord setter through the local bridge.
